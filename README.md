## About the Series

**Bleach** is a Japanese anime and manga series created by **Tite Kubo**. It follows *Ichigo Kurosaki*, a teenager who gains the powers of a **Soul Reaper** (Shinigami) and must protect the living world from evil spirits called **Hollows**.

## Main Characters

| Character | Role | Zanpakutō |
|-----------|------|-----------|

| Renji Abarai | Lieutenant, 6th Division | Zabimaru |
| Byakuya Kuchiki | Captain, 6th Division | Senbonzakura |
